User Guide

What NandaEdge Advisor Is — and Isn't

NandaEdge Advisor is a read-only market intelligence dashboard. It pulls live quotes, technicals, options chains, and statistical forecasts, then packages them into plain-English trade ideas. Think of it as a research analyst that never sleeps — it surfaces what looks interesting and why, but every decision (and every actual trade) stays with you.

New: Beginner Mode, Collapsible Sections & Toolbar

Two view modes sit in the tab bar. Beginner hides the heavy analytics (forecasts, options flow, risk, day-trade) so you see just the essentials; Advanced shows everything. Click any section title to collapse or expand it — the layout remembers your choices. Hover the small “?” icons for instant explanations of any term.

  • 📊 Compare — pick 2–4 watchlist tickers and see signal, RSI, trend, and rating side by side.
  • 🔔 Alerts — set price-target alerts; the badge flags any that have triggered against live prices.
  • 📓 Journal — log your trades with a rationale, mark win/loss, and track your win rate over time.
  • 📅 Calendar — earnings, Fed meetings, data releases, and options-expiry dates that can move your holdings.

How to Read a Stock Card

Each stock card is a mini research report, top to bottom:

  • Header — price, day change, analyst tip, price target, and short interest.
  • SA Factor Grades — Value, Growth, Profitability, Momentum, Revisions (A = best, F = worst).
  • Technical Snapshot — trend, RSI, MACD, Bollinger, VWAP, ATR, support/resistance.
  • Swing Trade — a 2–10 day plan: entry zone, two targets, and a stop, derived from ATR and S/R.
  • Options Play — a suggested strategy with strikes, expiry/DTE, and the Greeks.
  • AI Trade Intelligence — a blended read: confidence, risk, suggested DTE, win probability.

Signals, Scores & the Color Scale

Wherever you see a confidence or quality score, the color tells you the read at a glance — higher is better:

  • Green (70+) — strong, well-aligned setup.
  • Yellow (55–69) — mixed; some signals conflict.
  • Orange (45–54) — weak; caution warranted.
  • Red (<45) — poor alignment; likely avoid.

★ Stars rate overall conviction (1–5). Smart Score (0–10) blends technicals, analyst view, and momentum. Signal (Buy/Hold/Sell) is the headline call. Price change uses classic green-up / red-down and is separate from the quality scale.

Market & Macro Regime

The macro panel is the weather report for the whole market — it sets the odds before any single stock:

  • VIX Regime — the market's fear gauge. Calm = strategies work as expected; spiking = defensive.
  • Risk-On / Risk-Off — are investors chasing growth, or fleeing to safety (bonds, cash)?
  • Yield Curve — 2yr vs 10yr gap. Flat/inverted often precedes slowdowns; steep = expansion.
  • Fed Stance — Hold / Hike / Cut. Cuts are usually a tailwind for stocks.
  • Cycle Stage — Early → Mid → Late → Recession; different sectors lead each stage.

Price Forecast (GBM) Explained

Geometric Brownian Motion is a standard statistical model that projects a range of future prices from a stock's historical daily returns and volatility. The table shows expected moves over 1 week to 6 months, color-shaded by magnitude (green up, red down).

  • It is a probability estimate, not a prediction — the future path is uncertain.
  • Wider ranges mean higher volatility (more risk and more opportunity).
  • Best used to sanity-check targets and size positions, not as a guarantee.

Options Strategies, in Plain English

  • Cash-Secured Put — you get paid a premium for agreeing to buy the stock cheaper. Mildly bullish / income.
  • Covered Call — you own the stock and sell a call for income, capping upside. Neutral / income.
  • Bull Call Spread — buy a call, sell a higher one; cheaper, defined-risk bullish bet.
  • Long Call — leveraged bullish bet; higher risk, higher reward.
  • Iron Condor — profits if the stock stays in a range. Neutral / low-volatility.

DTE = days to expiration. IV Rank = how expensive options are vs their own history (high IV favors selling premium).

Glossary — Key Terms

  • RSI (14) — momentum 0–100. Above 70 overbought, below 30 oversold.
  • MACD — trend-momentum crossover; bull cross = upward shift, bear cross = downward.
  • VWAP — volume-weighted average price; above = intraday strength.
  • ATR (14) — average daily range in dollars; a volatility / stop-sizing gauge.
  • Bollinger Bands — volatility envelope; a “squeeze” often precedes a big move.
  • EMA 9/20/50/200 — moving averages; stacked upward = healthy uptrend.
  • Support / Resistance — price floors and ceilings from recent history.
  • Delta / Theta / Vega — option sensitivity to price / time-decay / volatility.
  • Short Interest — % of shares sold short; high = crowded bearish bet (squeeze risk).
  • Analyst PT — a firm's 12-month price target.
